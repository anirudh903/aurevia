ABOUT AUREVIA ATELIER

Where Vision
Meets Timeless Design

Aurevia Atelier is a multidisciplinary interior design studio based in Mumbai, creating bespoke spaces that reflect your personality, purpose and lifestyle. We believe that every space has the power to inspire, uplift and leave a lasting legacy. Led by founder and principal designer Jaina Mehta, the studio pairs a considered eye with hands-on craft on every project.
